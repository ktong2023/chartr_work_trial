Clinical determinations follow the CDC 2021 Sexually Transmitted Infections Treatment Guidelines and the CDC 2024 doxycycline post-exposure prophylaxis guidelines. Clinic instructions in the chart and in clinic documents govern where they apply. Documentation dated after the evaluation time has no effect.

Statuses (per checkpoint):
- `completed`: a qualifying result satisfies the checkpoint.
- `overdue`: treatment is complete, the checkpoint is not completed, and it was due on or before the evaluation date.
- `not_due`: treatment is not complete; or treatment is complete, the checkpoint is not completed, and it is due after the evaluation date.
- `blocked`: treatment is complete and the checkpoint depends on an earlier checkpoint that is not completed.
- `cannot_determine`: not completed, and a result that is referenced but not in the chart could have completed it.
- `unclear`: the governing plan, schedule, or treatment completion cannot be established from the record.
- `no_requirement`: no follow-up plan applies.
